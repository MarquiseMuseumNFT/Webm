## A4-FM-001 — PBEC experiential-yield functional

**Source family:** `004:071`  
**Status:** `FORMALIZATION-CANDIDATE`  
**Novelty state:** paper-kernel provisional; exact 104 occupancy unresolved.

Let
- `q(t)` = candidate-experience presentation rate;
- `p_M(t)` = fraction of presented experiences lying inside the personally meaningful preference manifold;
- `u(F,I,C)` = immediate utility of fidelity `F`, interactivity `I`, and consequence/commitment `C`;
- `n(t)` = remaining novelty fraction.

Formalization:

`Y(T) = ∫_0^T q(t) p_M(t) u(F(t),I(t),C(t)) n(t) dt`

Interpretation: generable content supply is not the operative denominator. Long-run experiential yield is filtered by personal relevance and novelty depletion. The source-derived mechanism is stronger than the equation; the equation is an operational sketch, not a literal source claim.

**Prediction:** increasing fidelity may increase `u` while also accelerating novelty depletion in `n`, so long-run `Y(T)` can be non-monotonic in fidelity.

**Failure / collision test:** if the 104 registry already contains the same joint mechanism — personal meaningful manifold + fidelity-driven saturation + augmentation denominator drift — do not promote PBEC as a new paper.
